How to use these rates with your records

These are annual allowance thresholds, subject to CRA conditions. Rivo reports use the rate configured for the driver; the app does not automatically apply these annual tiers. Self-employed vehicle deductions generally use eligible actual expenses and business-use records instead of a blanket per-kilometre deduction.
