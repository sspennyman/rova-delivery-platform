Questions teams ask before switching

Clear answers before rollout.

How does a driver create an account?

Dispatch adds the driver’s email and Rivo creates a secure invitation. The driver opens it once, chooses a password, and signs in by email from then on. Dispatch can issue a new link for an expired invitation or password reset.

Can Rivo route several orders for one driver?

Yes. Select multiple orders for a driver, sequence the stops with route optimization when Google Routes is configured, preview the route, and drag active stops into a different order before opening navigation.

Can we use our own drivers and third-party couriers?

Yes. Assign selected orders to an in-house driver or a saved courier partner. Rivo keeps the courier request, status, reference number, fee, and tracking link beside the order. Automatic email sending requires a configured account sender and an email request method for that partner; otherwise dispatch can open or copy the prepared request, clearly marked as not sent.

What proof is saved at delivery?

Drivers can take or choose a photo, collect a recipient signature, enter who received the order, and add a note. Rivo also records the outcome, time, driver, latest trip location when available, and any structured delivery issue for dispatch review.

Will drivers be tracked all day?

No. Location sharing is tied to active trip work and can be paused. The driver screen shows when sharing is on, while dispatch sees the latest update for active deliveries.

What can connect to the order queue?

Rivo supports Shopify, WooCommerce, and Wix connections, plus a universal webhook for Squarespace, Square, Make, Zapier, custom sites, and other systems that can send order data.

Can we include paid home and return trips?

Yes. Save a driver’s home address, create Home → Work and Depot → Home legs, and keep those business trips in the same mileage and weekly approval flow.

Do we need to replace our website?

No. Rivo sits behind the storefront or order source you already use. Customers keep buying through your brand while paid delivery orders move into the dispatch queue.
