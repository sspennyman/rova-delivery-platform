Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your Rivo account and associated personal data from the website or mobile app.

  1. Sign in to your Rivo account.
  2. Drivers: open Settings in the app navigation. Workspace owners: open workspace settings, then Support.
  3. Choose Request account deletion, enter your current password, and type DELETE.

Sign in to request deletion

What happens next

Your request is recorded with a reference number and remains pending manual processing. Submitting a request does not immediately delete your account or stop tracking. Pause Auto-track in Settings if you want recording to stop now.

The request covers your account profile and associated personal data, including private trip records. Shared business reports and delivery records require a retention review. Your account remains active until processing is complete.

If you cannot sign in, contact hello@floraljet.llc. Do not send your password or GPS history by email.
